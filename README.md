# The Great Emu War

A Galaga-style arcade shooter set in the 1932 Great Emu War in Western Australia. You play the soldiers in a truck with a Lewis gun. You defend the wheat at Campion for as many days as you can. The emus get faster, tougher and more numerous every day, so you will lose. The question is how high you can score before you do.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It's a single file with no build step and no dependencies. Fonts load from Google Fonts and fall back cleanly if you're offline.

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | ← → or A / D | Drag |
| Fire | Space (or Z / J / ↑), hold for auto | Hold |
| Reload early / clear a jam | R (mash to clear a jam faster) | Tap a second finger |
| Pause | P or Esc | Pause button |
| Sound | M | Sound button |

## How it plays

- **Days.** Each wave is one day of the campaign, starting 2 November 1932. Before each day you get a dispatch card with a real fact or quote from the campaign.
- **Formation and dives.** Emus fly in on looping paths, settle into formation, then break off and charge the truck while kicking up dirt clods. Shooting them mid-charge is worth double.
- **Black-plumed leaders** take two hits and charge with escorts. This comes from Meredith's report about each pack having "a big black-plumed bird" as its leader.
- **Helmeted emus** show up from day 4 and take an extra hit ("the invulnerability of tanks").
- **Guerrilla tactics.** From day 6, shooting a bird in formation can make its neighbours break ranks and charge.
- **The Lewis gun.** A pan holds 47 rounds. Hold the trigger too long and the gun overheats and jams.
- **Wheat.** Every emu that gets past the truck eats some of the crop. You lose when the wheat is gone or you run out of trucks. Wheat left at the end of a day is a harvest bonus.
- **Ambush at the dam.** Days 3, 7, 11 and so on are bonus stages. The emus won't attack, and you score 100 per bird, or 10,000 if you get all 40.
- **Crates.** T is twin guns, C is a cold barrel (no heat, instant reload), W restores wheat and + is a spare truck. You also get extra trucks at 20,000 and 60,000 points, then every 60,000.
- **The real ending.** The game-over report compares your rounds per emu with Meredith's official tally: 986 birds for 9,860 rounds.

High scores are saved in your browser's local storage.
